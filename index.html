nLoop Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

This policy explains how nLoop handles information when you use the nLoop app. nLoop is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13.

Information stored on your device

nLoop stores your followed topics and priorities, saved-story IDs, and a cached briefing in the app’s storage on your device. nLoop does not send these preferences, saved items, or your reading history to our server. This information remains on your device until you clear nLoop’s app storage or uninstall the app.

Connections to nLoop

The app connects to nLoop’s server hosted on Oracle Cloud to retrieve the same shared briefing for all users and to check for signed app updates. nLoop does not ask you to create an account and does not receive your email address, topic choices, saved stories, or a record of which stories you read.

Like other internet services, Oracle’s servers and hosting infrastructure can receive your device’s IP address, connection time, requested service, and basic technical request information. We use service logs to operate, secure, and troubleshoot nLoop. They are not used to identify you or build a user profile, and are removed through routine log rotation. Oracle may separately process infrastructure data under its privacy practices.

Briefing content and AI

nLoop collects publicly available article feeds and uses an AI model running on our Oracle server to prepare shared briefings. The app does not send your personal information or reading history to the AI model. Briefing and article data on the server is shared across users and is not associated with an individual account.

Article images and links

Some story cards load images directly from the publisher’s website. If you open an original story, nLoop opens that publisher’s page in your browser. Those publishers may receive your IP address and technical request information, and handle it under their own privacy policies. nLoop does not receive their browsing records.

Advertising, analytics, and sharing

nLoop does not include advertising or analytics and does not sell personal information. We do not share a user profile with third parties. Oracle provides hosting for the nLoop server; publishers provide linked stories and images as described above.

Retention and deletion

Device-stored preferences, saved items, and cached briefings can be removed by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling nLoop. nLoop has no user accounts or server-side reading profiles to delete. Shared article and briefing content is retained on the server as needed to operate the service and is not linked to a user. Technical service logs are retained only for operations and security and are removed through routine rotation. If you believe a service log contains information about you, contact us below.

Security

Connections between nLoop and its server use HTTPS. We limit server access and use reasonable measures to protect information handled by the service. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as nLoop changes. The effective date above shows when it was last revised. For privacy questions or requests, contact asaarmando05@gmail.com.
